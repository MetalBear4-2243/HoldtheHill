### Towers

**TW-3. After every build the tower card opens over the map while the ghost stays armed; build clicks there hit Upgrade or Sell** (medium; where: PR #38, #39, #40, review-fixes)
Meadow at 1920x1080: press 1 and build an Ant on (-4,-2). The new Ant is selected, so the card opens bottom-left and covers about 3x3 cells beside the road corner (-6,-3). The Ant ghost is still armed. A click on the buildable cell (-7,-3) lands on Upgrade: no Ant is built and the first one is upgraded for 45. A later click on (-7,-4) lands on Sell and sells it (90 after that upgrade) with no confirmation. Drops under the card can't be collected. Every build reopens the card. Cause: `_Game/Features/Towers/Scripts/TowerPlacer.cs:388` calls `SelectTower(tower)` and never `Select(-1)`; the card (`_Game/Scenes/UI.unity:5415-5416`, 360x358 at (24,24), raycast target) swallows the click (`TowerPlacer.cs:329-330`). Fix: don't open the card on a build while a tower stays in hand. Drop `SelectTower(tower)` from `PlaceTower`, or disarm and then select. Longer term, keep the card off the playfield. PU-1 has the same root.

**TW-1. The card's Sell label keeps the pre-upgrade refund after every upgrade** (medium; where: PR #38, #39, #40, review-fixes)
Between waves (no bounties arrive), build an Ant for 75: the card reads "Sell +56". Upgrade for 45: it still reads +56, but a sale pays 90. After the next upgrade (70) it reads +90 and pays 142. It catches up only on the next Honeydew change, a burrow toggle, an order click or reselecting the tower. The refund paid is right; the label is always too low. Cause: `_Game/Features/Towers/Scripts/TowerPlacer.cs:236-237` calls `Upgrade()`, which raises `Upgraded` and redraws the card, before it adds the cost to `HoneydewSpent`. Fix: `_selectedTower.HoneydewSpent += cost;` before `_selectedTower.Upgrade();` (a Ready verdict means `Upgrade` succeeds). Add a test that `SellValue` already includes the upgrade when `Upgraded` fires.

**TW-11 (merged with UI-5 and CT-2). Enter on a still-selected tower-card button upgrades, sells or burrows while paused or after the run ends** (medium; UI-5 and CT-2 rated it low; where: TowerCardPanel PR #38, #40; TowerPlacer PR #38, #39, #40, review-fixes; LevelRun PR #38, #39, review-fixes)

When the pause or result panel shows, select its first button so Enter means Resume or Restart. TW-11 also notes that `TowerPlacer.cs` is 401 lines and mixes input, markers, building, selection, upgrade, sell and burrow-all; split selection, upgrade and sell into a `TowerSelection` component later (improvement 21).

**TW-6. Fire rate drops at 2x and at low frame rates because the cooldown throws away each frame's overshoot** (low; where: PR #36, #38, #39, #40, review-fixes)
On a 60 Hz screen at 2x, a frame is about 0.033 s. A level-1 Crazy Ant (0.35 s) bites every 11 frames (0.367 s): about 4.5% fewer bites. Level 3 loses about 6.25%, and with Swarm Frenzy about 12.5%. At 30 fps the level-1 loss at 2x is 12.5%. Enemies still move their full distance, so fast melee towers get weaker at 2x. Long-interval towers lose 2% or less. Cause: `_Game/Features/Towers/Scripts/Tower.cs:202` sets `_cooldown` instead of adding to it; `MineLayer.cs:68, 77` do the same. Fix: `_cooldown = Mathf.Max(_cooldown, -Time.deltaTime) + _fireInterval / TowerTempo.FireRateFactor;` in both.

Correct the comment (75 + 45 + 70 = 190 gives 142).
